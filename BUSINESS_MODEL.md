# Business Model Plan - SomDun

## Overview
- Product: Calorie tracking app with AI meal analysis, barcode scanning, and gamified progress.
- Core value: Fast logging + actionable insights + habit reinforcement.
- Primary market: Thailand / SEA, health and fitness focused users.

## Target Users
- Individuals managing weight and nutrition.
- Gym-goers and athletes tracking macros.
- Beginners who need simple guidance and motivation.

## Value Proposition
- AI photo and barcode logging reduces friction.
- Macro tracking with goals and daily summary.
- Gamified player card and streak mechanics improve retention.

## Revenue Model
- Freemium + paid subscription.
- B2B partnerships with gyms, coaches, and wellness programs.

## Pricing (Example)
### Free
- Manual logging, basic macros, daily summary.
- Limited AI scans (e.g., 3 per day).

### Pro (US$6-10/mo, THB 149-299)
- Unlimited AI scans.
- Advanced insights and trends.
- Goal suggestion, exports, premium themes.

### Coach / Team (B2B)
- Coach dashboard for client management.
- Progress summaries and exports.
- Team billing and seat management.

## Go-To-Market
- Short-form video content (TikTok/IG) focused on scan-to-log.
- Local influencer partnerships in fitness and nutrition.
- Referral loop: extra AI scans or streak boosts.
- App store optimization for local search keywords.
- B2B pilots with gyms and nutritionists.

## Unit Economics
- Primary costs: AI inference, storage, hosting, payment fees.
- Controls: scan limits, caching, photo compression, prompt optimization.

## Key Metrics
- Activation: 3 meals logged within first 3 days.
- Retention: D7/D30 logging streaks.
- Conversion: Free to Pro, Pro churn rate.
- ARPU, CAC, LTV.

## Risks & Mitigation
- AI cost spikes: cap free usage, optimize inference.
- Accuracy trust: confidence ranges + disclaimers.
- Competition: focus on local food knowledge + personalization.

## 12-Month Roadmap
- Q1: onboarding polish, local food dataset, referral loop.
- Q2: subscription rollout, advanced insights.
- Q3: coach dashboard + B2B pilots.
- Q4: regional expansion and partnerships.
